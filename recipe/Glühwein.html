Glühwein

This Glühwein recipe makes a hot spiced "glow wine" that's found in many winter markets in Germany and Austria.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup water
  • ¾ cup white sugar
  • 1 (3-inch) cinnamon stick
  • 2 medium oranges, divided
  • 10 whole cloves
  • 1 (750 milliliter) bottle red wine
