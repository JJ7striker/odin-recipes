Savory Italian Pretzels

Often served with (and dipped in!) white wine, taralli are a fun and versatile Italian snack you can flavor all sorts of ways.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ cup white wine
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ½ cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
